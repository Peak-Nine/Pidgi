01
The Vosges · Le Valtinfr
First base · the warm-up

Forest, ridges, a string of glacier lakes

drive in 5u22 Wed 15 → Sat 18 · 3 nights

You start gently. Rounded green mountains, deep pine, and a ridge road called the Route des Crêtes that runs along the top like a balcony. On a clear day you can see the Black Forest, and sometimes the Alps far off, which is a nice promise of what is coming.

Le Valtin sits right under the Col de la Schlucht, the highest pass in the Vosges. So the good stuff is on the doorstep.

foresteasy hikeslakescheese country
on the ground
  • Walk the Sentier des Roches from the Col de la Schlucht, a rocky path with metal staircases and big views (skip it if anyone hates heights).
  • Do the four-lake walk: Lac Vert, Lac des Truites, Lac Noir and Lac Blanc, looping back along the open ridge.
  • Climb the Hohneck (1363m), the highest summit around, for a 360° look over Alsace.
  • Lunch at a ferme-auberge (a working farm inn) and try the local Munster cheese.
  • Late afternoon is best for spotting chamois on the ridges. Guided evening walks exist if you want help finding them.
  • Easy day: Lac de Gérardmer nearby for a swim or a pedalo, plus the old water-powered sawmill at Le Lançoir.
02
Obersee · Lake Constancede
Second base · the big lake

A wide summer lake and an old town

drive in 3u22 Sat 18 → Mon 20 · 2 nights

This is the water stop. The Obersee is the big open basin of Lake Constance, the lake where Germany, Switzerland and Austria all meet. You settle near Konstanz, an old university town sitting right where the lake pinches in.

Warm, easy days. Swimming, ferries, and ice cream on the promenade.

big lakeswimmingferriesold town
on the ground
  • Wander Konstanz old town and harbour, past the slowly turning Imperia statue at the water's edge.
  • Spend an afternoon on Mainau, the island of flowers and gardens, a short hop away.
  • Take the ferry across to Meersburg, a pretty medieval town under its old castle.
  • Swim from one of the lakeside lidos, or rent a paddleboat or SUP; the water is warm in summer.
  • Easy day trips: the Reichenau island gardens, or the Rhine Falls near Schaffhausen, about an hour off.
03
Vaduz · Liechtensteinli
Third base · the curiosity

A whole country, mountains and all

drive in 1u15 Mon 20 → Wed 22 · 2 nights

The short hop. Liechtenstein is tiny, tucked between Switzerland and Austria along the Rhine, and you can get the measure of it in a day. The prince's castle stands on a crag right over the capital, which gives the whole place a fairytale opening.

Then it surprises you, because behind the small valley there are real, serious mountains.

tiny countrycastleridge hikea curiosity
on the ground
  • Look up at Vaduz Castle (the royal residence, so admired from outside) and wander the small capital and its art museums.
  • Drive up to Malbun, the little mountain village, for easy walks and a chairlift.
  • For the sure-footed: the Fürstensteig, the country's famous ridge path. It is exposed and only for experienced walkers, so check conditions first.
  • Visit Gutenberg Castle in Balzers, the one you can actually walk into.
  • Silly and fun: get a souvenir passport stamp, then say you spent two nights in a principality.
04
Lago Maggiore · Stresait
Fourth base · the heart of it

Italy, by boat, with the Alps behind

drive in 3u00 Wed 22 → Fri 24 · 2 nights

This is the payoff. You come down out of the mountains into warmth: palm trees on the promenade, faded grand hotels, baroque palaces sitting on islands in the middle of a blue lake. Stresa is the base, and the boats do the work from there.

If the trip has a postcard, it is here.

warmthboatsgardensaperitivothe romantic one
on the ground
  • Ferry to the Borromean Islands: Isola Bella for the baroque palace and ten terraced gardens (peacocks included), Isola Madre for the botanical gardens, and Isola dei Pescatori, a tiny fishing village, for lunch.
  • Take the Mottarone cable car up for a view over the lake and a row of peaks.
  • Boat to Santa Caterina del Sasso, a monastery built into a cliff over the water.
  • Wander the Villa Taranto gardens across the bay in Verbania.
  • Then do nothing useful at all: swim, kayak, and an aperitivo on the promenade as the light goes. A sunset cruise if you feel like it.
05
Dijon · Bourgognefr
Fifth base · the soft landing

A good dinner before the last drive

drive in 6u30 Fri 24 → Sat 25 · 1 night

One night to land softly. Dijon is the golden-stone capital of Burgundy, all old streets and good food, and just south of it the great wine road begins. So you finish the trip the right way, with a proper meal and a glass of something local.

foodwineold townone last night
on the ground
  • Walk the old town and the Palais des Ducs, following the brass owl trail. Rub the little stone owl on Notre-Dame for luck on the road home.
  • Buy proper Dijon mustard at the Maille shop, and eat at Les Halles, the covered market.
  • If there's time before the drive: a slow morning down the Route des Grands Crus through Gevrey-Chambertin and Nuits-Saint-Georges toward Beaune.
  • Order the classics: boeuf bourguignon, escargots, a kir. You earned it.
⌂

Then home, about 5u30, with the whole loop behind you.

Forest to mirror lake to a tiny kingdom to an Italian island lunch to a Burgundy table. Five very different countries, and you sleep in your own bed on Saturday night.
